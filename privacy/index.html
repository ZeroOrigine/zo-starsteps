Privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026

In short
  • Children can play with no account. Then nothing about them leaves the device.
  • If a parent creates an account, we store the parent's email and, for each child, a first name, a grade and their game progress. Nothing else about children.
  • We never ask children for an email, photo, location, voice or contact details, and we never show ads.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we use no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • A parent can see, download or delete everything at any time in the parent area.

Without a parent account none of this is sent to us. To erase it, open You in the game and choose Start over, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What we count

When the Star Steps page loads, our server adds one to a tally for that day and country. The tally records the date and a two-letter country code. It does not record a name, an IP address, a device identifier or a cookie, so it cannot tell who opened the app or link one visit to another.

Our hosting provider

The site is served by Netlify. Like every web host, Netlify receives each visitor's IP address in order to deliver the page and may keep it in its own server logs for security and operations, under Netlify's privacy policy. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.

Children

Star Steps is made for children aged 5 to 12. We do not ask children for an email address, a photo, a location or any other personal information, and we do not show them advertising. Plans, settings and the parent area sit behind a question meant for grown-ups, and only a parent can create an account or buy a plan.

Parent accounts

A parent account is optional. It is for a parent or legal guardian aged 18 or older, who creates it and adds the children. Because the parent sets up each child profile, the parent gives consent for the little we keep about that child.

We do not use children's information for advertising, profiling or anything other than running Star Steps for your family. Account emails are only about your account and plan: confirming your email, password resets, trial and payment notices. No marketing.

Payments

Paid plans are handled by Stripe. You type your card details on Stripe's secure page; we never see or store your full card number. Stripe tells us the plan, its status and the last payment result.

Who helps us run Star Steps

We use a few service providers, each only for its job and under its own privacy terms: Netlify (hosting the site), Supabase (the account database, hosted in Canada), Stripe (payments) and Resend (sending account emails). None of them may use your family's information for their own purposes.

Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections. Each family can only ever read its own data: the database enforces this on every request. Passwords are checked against known data breaches when they are set.

Your choices

In the parent area you can see each child's progress, change a name or grade, remove a child, download all of your family's data as a file, and delete your account. Deleting the account removes the parent account, every child profile and all progress straight away, and cancels any plan. Progress saved on a device stays there until you choose Start over or clear the browser's data. You can also email us to ask for any of this.
