Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026

In short
  • Star Steps does not collect personal information from children or from anyone else.
  • Your child's progress is saved in the browser on your device and is never uploaded.
  • There are no ads, no cookies and no third-party trackers.
  • We count how many times the app is opened each day and in which country, and nothing more.

What stays on your device

WhatWhyWhere
The first name your child typesSo Pip can greet themThis browser only
Progress, stars, streaks and rewardsSo the game remembers where they areThis browser only
Settings such as language, sound and quiet modeSo choices stickThis browser only

None of this is sent to us. To erase it, open You in the game and choose Start over, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What we count

When the Star Steps page loads, our server adds one to a tally for that day and country. The tally records the date and a two-letter country code. It does not record a name, an IP address, a device identifier or a cookie, so it cannot tell who opened the app or link one visit to another.

Our hosting provider

The site is served by Netlify. Like every web host, Netlify receives each visitor's IP address in order to deliver the page and may keep it in its own server logs for security and operations, under Netlify's privacy policy. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.

Children

Star Steps is made for children aged 5 to 12. We do not ask children for an email address, a photo, a location or any other personal information, and we do not show them advertising. Plans and settings sit behind a question meant for grown-ups.

Parent accounts

Parent accounts are not available yet. Before they launch, this page will explain exactly what an account stores, who can see it, how long it is kept and how to delete it, and nothing will be collected before then.

Contact

Questions about privacy: reply@zeroorigine.com. Star Steps is looked after as part of ZeroOrigine.
